A luxury villa deserves a website to match

Guests renting a villa compare exceptional places. What decides them is being able to picture themselves there: breakfast by the pool, sunset from the terrace. An immersive website gives them that picture in a few seconds. And on multi-night stays, direct bookings make a real difference for you.

Your villa website includes

  • The immersive film made from your real photos.
  • Every space: bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, outdoor areas, pool.
  • Services: cleaning, private chef, transfers, concierge, if you offer them.
  • The area: beaches, villages, golf, restaurants.
  • Direct booking requests by WhatsApp, phone or contact form.
  • Several languages for international guests.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.
  2. We prepare your preview.Your photos become a film that moves forward as visitors scroll, with your texts, your rooms or your services.
  3. You approve it.You look at the preview and ask for any tweaks you like.
  4. Your site is online within 72 hours.On yourname.immerweb.com or your own domain, ready for Google and perfect on mobile.

What direct bookings change for you

Since autumn 2026, Airbnb keeps 15.5% on the host side (airbnb.com/help/article/1857). Abritel/Vrbo keeps 12% since 29 October 2026. On a 3-night stay at €120 a night, a booking made directly on your website leaves you €55.80 more than an Airbnb booking.

Platforms remain great shop windows. Your website welcomes the guests who found you, the ones who come back and the ones who heard about you from friends: they book with you, by phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your booking engine.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rent out my villa directly?

With a website in your name that makes people dream and an instant contact (WhatsApp, phone, contact form). You can keep platforms to be discovered and welcome loyal and recommended guests directly.

Can the site be in other languages for my foreign guests?

Yes: English, French, Spanish and other languages on request.

Is the film true to my villa?

Yes. It always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing any room or view.

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Can I keep my own web address?

Yes. Your site is published on yourname.immerweb.com and can run on your own domain name: we guide you step by step, it takes about 10 minutes.

You are free to stop whenever you want, with a simple email. Your site stays online at its address: only SEO, edits and updates stop.
