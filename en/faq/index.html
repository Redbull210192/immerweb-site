Immerweb in brief

What is Immerweb?

Immerweb builds immersive websites: the photos of a place become a film that moves forward as you scroll the page, and the whole website is built around it (rooms or services, gallery, contact, direct booking). Immerweb is a trading name of RBBLG LIMITED, a company registered in the United Kingdom. Contact: contact@immerweb.com.

Who is Immerweb for?

Bed and breakfasts, holiday cottages, guesthouses, hotels and boutique hotels, chalets, villas, vacation rentals, campsites, glamping and cabins, and also restaurants, shops, craftspeople, studios and any local business with a place to show.

Where can I see an example?

The live EXO demo: immerweb.com/demo/exo/. A client hotel website: quattrocuori.immerweb.com.

Does Immerweb also build classic websites?

Yes, if you prefer a website without the film, at the same price: €290 then €29 per month.

Pricing

How much does a website for a holiday cottage or B&B cost?

With Immerweb: €290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. From a web agency, a showcase website costs a median of €5,200 (La Fabrique du Net, July 2026), plus €80 to €150 per month for maintenance.

What does the €29 monthly plan include?

Hosting, a yourname.immerweb.com address (or your own domain), edits to texts, photos and rates, seasonal updates, and SEO for Google and AI-powered search engines.

Can the €29 price go up?

It stays locked for life as long as your plan continues.

Can I stop the monthly plan?

Yes, you are free to stop whenever you want, with a simple email. Your site stays online at its address; only SEO, edits and updates stop.

How do I pay?

Online, by card, through Stripe. The €290 is paid when you order, and the monthly plan starts on day 31.

Timing and how it works

How quickly will my website be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos.

What do I need to provide?

A link to your photos: your current website, your Google listing, your Instagram, or phone photos. You first receive a free preview.

Are the videos made up?

The film always starts from your real photos; artificial intelligence adds camera movement and light, without creating fake places.

Can I keep my domain name?

Yes. The site is published on yourname.immerweb.com and can run on your own domain by changing two settings with your provider; we guide you.

Can my website be multilingual?

Yes: English, French, Spanish and other languages on request.

How do guests book?

By phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your current booking engine: we connect what you already use. A custom built-in booking system is quoted separately.

Direct bookings and platforms

How do I get more direct bookings?

Have a website in your own name that ranks well, makes people want to stay within seconds, offers instant contact (WhatsApp, phone, form), and invite your guests to come back direct. According to Expedia Group (2025, 7,000 travellers), 71% of travellers say a video influences their choice, versus 24% for photos.

What commission does Airbnb take in 2026?

Since autumn 2026, Airbnb keeps 15.5% on the host side (airbnb.com/help/article/1857).

What commission do Abritel and Vrbo take?

12% since 29 October 2026.

How much more does a direct booking earn me?

On a 3-night stay at €120 a night, booking direct leaves the host €55.80 more than an Airbnb booking.

Should I leave Airbnb or Booking.com?

Keep them: platforms help you get discovered, and your website welcomes loyal guests, recommended guests and people who search for your name.

Choosing your website

What is the best website for a bed and breakfast?

A website in your own name, beautiful on mobile, that makes people feel the place (a film rather than a simple gallery), ranks well on Google and lets guests book direct. That is what Immerweb offers for €290 then €29 per month.

What is the difference between an immersive website and a classic one?

On a classic website, you see still photos. On an immersive website, a film in one continuous shot moves forward as you scroll: visitors feel as if they are touring the place.

Is an immersive website good for SEO?

Yes: beneath the film, the site keeps real text, headings, tags and structured data that Google and AI assistants can read.

Do I need a website if I already have a Google listing and Instagram?

They help people discover you; your website brings everything together, builds trust and receives direct bookings. Your Google listing and Instagram point to it.
