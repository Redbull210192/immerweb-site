A boutique hotel is chosen on emotion

A guest hesitating between three hotels is mostly comparing a feeling. The décor, the light, the mood of the place: that is what sets you apart from the big chains. An immersive website conveys that feeling in a few seconds, because the visitor is no longer flicking through photos one by one: they are moving through your hotel.

A real example: the website of the boutique hotel Quattrocuori, built by Immerweb.

What your hotel website includes

  • The opening film made from your real photos.
  • Rooms and suites, each with photos, size, view and amenities.
  • Your services: restaurant, bar, spa, pool, parking, meetings and events.
  • The gallery and your neighbourhood or region.
  • Your booking engine connected to the site, or phone, WhatsApp and a contact form.
  • Several languages for your international guests.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.

Frequently asked questions

Can my booking engine be connected to the website?

Yes. We connect what you already use: booking engine, phone, WhatsApp or contact form. A custom built-in booking system is quoted separately.

Is it suitable for a small independent hotel?

That is exactly who it is designed for: a look worthy of the grand houses, at the price of a simple website, €290 then €29 per month.

Can you redo my hotel's existing website?

Yes. We start from your current photos and texts, prepare a free preview, and the new site can run on your current address.

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life.

Can my website be in several languages?

Yes: English, French, Spanish and other languages on request. Ideal for welcoming international guests.

Do I need professional photos?

Your current photos are enough: from your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. The film always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing any place.

Will my website rank well on Google?

It is built for Google from day one: readable text, tags, structured data, Search Console. SEO work continues every month with the plan.
