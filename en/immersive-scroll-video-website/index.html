What is an immersive website?

An immersive website replaces the big homepage photo with a film in one continuous shot. The film is split into frames that the page shows in step with scrolling: scroll down, the camera moves forward; scroll up, it moves back. Visitors feel as if they are walking into the place. Below it, you find a complete website: about, rooms or services, gallery, reviews, contact and booking.

Try it on the EXO demo or on the Immerweb homepage.

How we make your film

We always start from your real photos: from your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. Artificial intelligence adds camera movement and light between the images, without inventing any place. The result is a continuous shot true to your business, optimised to stay smooth and fast on mobile.

Frequently asked questions

Is an immersive website slow to load?

It is optimised for mobile: the first frames appear straight away and the rest loads while the visitor scrolls.

How is it different from an embedded YouTube video?

A video plays on its own. Here, the visitor steers the camera with a finger or a mouse wheel and moves through your place at their own pace.

Does this kind of website suit my business?

It suits any place worth visiting: B&Bs, holiday cottages, hotels, villas, campsites, but also restaurants, shops, craft workshops and studios.
