Frequently asked questions

How much does an agency website cost in 2026?

A showcase website from a web agency costs a median of €5,200 (La Fabrique du Net, July 2026), then €80 to €150 per month for maintenance.

Can I ask for changes like with an agency?

Yes, with a simple email: texts, photos, rates, seasonal content, up to three rounds of edits per month, included in the €29.

What if I need a custom feature?

New features (online shop, built-in booking, new pages) are quoted, as with an agency, but starting from a site that is already live.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Can I keep my own web address?

Yes. Your site is published on yourname.immerweb.com and can run on your own domain name: we guide you step by step, it takes about 10 minutes.

What if I want to stop the monthly plan?

You are free to stop whenever you want, with a simple email. Your site stays online at its address: only SEO, edits and updates stop.

What if I prefer a classic website, without the film?

That works too, at the same price: €290 then €29 per month. The immersive film is our signature; the choice is yours.
