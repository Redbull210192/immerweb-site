Let people feel the outdoors

Glamping and unusual stays are chosen on emotion: sleeping in the trees, waking up facing the lake, dinner around the fire. A film that moves as you scroll conveys that feeling far better than a row of thumbnails. The visitor follows the path, discovers the accommodation and steps inside.

Your whole site on one website

  • The opening film made from your real photos.
  • Every type of stay: cabins, lodges, domes, yurts, mobile homes, tents, pitches.
  • Facilities: pool, washrooms, shop, activities, entertainment.
  • Season and rates, updated every year.
  • Booking: your booking engine connected to the site, or phone, WhatsApp and contact form.
  • Several languages for campers from abroad.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.
  2. We prepare your preview.Your photos become a film that moves forward as visitors scroll, with your texts, your rooms or your services.
  3. You approve it.You look at the preview and ask for any tweaks you like.
  4. Your site is online within 72 hours.On yourname.immerweb.com or your own domain, ready for Google and perfect on mobile.

From a few cabins to a large campsite

Whether you have three cabins or a large campsite, the price stays the same: €290 one-off, then €29 per month. The site grows with you: a new cabin, a new activity, new photos, we add them.

What direct bookings change for you

Since autumn 2026, Airbnb keeps 15.5% on the host side (airbnb.com/help/article/1857). Abritel/Vrbo keeps 12% since 29 October 2026. On a 3-night stay at €120 a night, a booking made directly on your website leaves you €55.80 more than an Airbnb booking.

Platforms remain great shop windows. Your website welcomes the guests who found you, the ones who come back and the ones who heard about you from friends: they book with you, by phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your booking engine.

Frequently asked questions

Can my campsite booking software be connected?

Yes, we connect what you already use: booking engine, phone, WhatsApp or contact form. A custom built-in booking system is quoted separately.

Does it suit a glamping site with just a few cabins?

Yes, it is ideal: each cabin gets its own page and the film shows off the natural setting.

Can I add new accommodation mid-season?

Yes. Send us the photos and details and we add it as part of your plan.

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Do I need professional photos?

Your current photos are enough: from your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. The film always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing any place.

Can my website be in several languages?

Yes: English, French, Spanish and other languages on request. Ideal for welcoming international guests.
