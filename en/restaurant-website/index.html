People choose a restaurant with their eyes

Before booking, people look: the mood of the dining room, the terrace, the dishes. An immersive website turns your photos into one continuous shot that moves through your restaurant, from the entrance to the kitchen. Visitors feel the atmosphere and want to take a seat.

What your restaurant website includes

  • The opening film made from your real photos.
  • Your menu and set menus, updated whenever you change them.
  • Opening hours, address, directions and a one-tap phone number.
  • Table booking: phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your online reservation tool.
  • Events and private hire, festive menus.
  • Your Google listing linked to the site.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.

Frequently asked questions

Can you put my menu online?

Yes, your menu and set menus are part of the site and updated with a simple email, as part of the plan.

Can my table booking tool be connected?

Yes, we connect what you already use: online reservation tool, phone, WhatsApp or contact form.

Does the website help me get found on Google Maps?

The site is linked to your Google listing and written for local SEO (neighbourhood, town, type of cuisine), which helps Google recommend you.

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Do I need professional photos?

Your current photos are enough: from your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. The film always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing any place.

What if I want to stop the monthly plan?

You are free to stop whenever you want, with a simple email. Your site stays online at its address: only SEO, edits and updates stop.
