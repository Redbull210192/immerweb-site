A chalet is rented on atmosphere

Guests who book a chalet are buying an atmosphere: the quiet, the snow, evenings by the fire, summer hikes. Your Immerweb site turns your photos into one continuous shot that starts outside, facing the mountains, and takes you inside the chalet. It has the effect of a video, and 71% of travellers say a video influences their choice, versus 24% for photos (Expedia Group, 2025).

Your chalet website includes

  • The immersive film made from your real photos, winter and summer.
  • Bedrooms and sleeping arrangements, plus the spa, sauna or hot tub if you have one.
  • Access: distance to the slopes, lifts and village, parking.
  • Both seasons: skiing in winter, hiking in summer, photos changed each season.
  • Direct booking: phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your booking engine.
  • Several languages for the international guests of ski resorts.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.

Frequently asked questions

Can the film show the chalet in winter and summer?

Yes, from your photos of each season. We can change the film or photos when the season turns; it is included in the plan.

My guests come from abroad. Can the site be translated?

Yes: English, French, Spanish and other languages on request, to welcome the international guests of mountain resorts.

Can I showcase several chalets?

Yes, each chalet gets its own page, photos and rates, on a website in your name.

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 one-off for the creation, then €29 per month for hosting, edits and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Do I need professional photos?

Your current photos are enough: from your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. The film always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing any place.

How do my guests book?

By phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your current booking engine: we connect what you already use. Bookings come straight to you.
