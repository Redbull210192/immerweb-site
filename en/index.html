Immerweb in a few words

Immerweb is a studio that turns the photos of a place into a scroll-driven film, then builds the whole website around it: rooms or services, gallery, contact and direct booking. We also build classic websites at the same price. Immerweb is a trading name of RBBLG LIMITED (United Kingdom). Contact: contact@immerweb.com.

What it costs

How much does an Immerweb website cost?

€290 + €29/month

€290 one-off for the creation: the immersive film made from your photos, every page (home, rooms or services, gallery, contact, legal pages), the mobile version and the Google setup.

Then €29 per month: hosting, a yourname.immerweb.com address or your own domain, edits to texts, photos and prices, seasonal updates and Google SEO. That €29 price is locked for life, and you are free to stop whenever you want.

For comparison: a showcase website from a web agency costs a median of €5,200 (La Fabrique du Net, July 2026), then €80 to €150 per month for maintenance.
