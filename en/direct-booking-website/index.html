How to get more direct bookings

  • Have a website in your own name that people find on Google when they search for you.
  • Make people want to stay straight away: 71% of travellers say a video influences their choice, versus 24% for photos (Expedia Group, 2025). Hence the immersive film.
  • Make contact instant: WhatsApp, phone, contact form, or your booking engine.
  • Invite guests to come back direct: add your website address to your welcome messages and cards.
  • Link your Google listing to your website.
