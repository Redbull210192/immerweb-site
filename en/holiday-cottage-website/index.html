Let guests tour your cottage before they arrive

A family choosing a self-catering cottage for a week wants to know where the children will sleep, whether the kitchen is well equipped, whether the garden is enclosed and what the view looks like. Your Immerweb site answers with images: the film enters the house and moves from room to room, like a real visit.

That impression matters: 71% of travellers say a video influences their choice, versus 24% for photos (Expedia Group, 2025, 7,000 travellers).

Your complete cottage website

  • The immersive film made from your real photos.
  • The house in detail: beds, equipment, linen, access, whether pets are welcome.
  • Several cottages? One page for each, with its own photos and rates.
  • Availability and rates updated every season.
  • Contact and direct booking: phone, WhatsApp, contact form or your booking engine.
  • The surroundings: activities, pubs and restaurants, markets, so guests can plan their stay.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a website for my holiday cottage easily?

Send us a link to your photos (listing, current website, Google or Instagram). We prepare a free preview, you approve it, and your site is online within 72 hours. Nothing to install.

How much does a website for a holiday cottage cost?

With Immerweb: €290 one-off, then €29 per month all-inclusive (hosting, edits, SEO). From a web agency, a showcase website costs a median of €5,200 (La Fabrique du Net, July 2026).

I have several cottages. Is that possible?

Yes. Each cottage gets its own page, photos and rates, all on one website in your name.

How quickly will my site be online?

Within 72 hours of receiving your photos. You first see a free preview, then we publish.

Can I keep my own web address?

Yes. Your site is published on yourname.immerweb.com and can run on your own domain name: we guide you step by step, it takes about 10 minutes.
