Your craft, in motion

A customer looking for a joiner, a furniture maker, a potter, a baker or an upholsterer wants to see the work and the person behind it. A film made from your real photos tells the story of your workshop: the tools, the gestures, the finished pieces. It is your shop window, open day and night.

The film always starts from your real photos: AI adds camera movement and light, without inventing work you have not done.

Your workshop website includes

  • The immersive film of your workshop or shop.
  • Your work in a gallery, organised the way you like.
  • Your services, the area you cover and your opening hours.
  • Direct contact: phone, WhatsApp, quote request form.
  • Your Google listing linked to the site, so people nearby find you.
  • Updates: new projects added with a simple email.

How it works

  1. You send your photos.From your website, your Google listing, Instagram or your phone. A link is enough.
  2. We prepare your preview.Your photos become a film that moves forward as visitors scroll, with your texts, your rooms or your services.
  3. You approve it.You look at the preview and ask for any tweaks you like.
  4. Your site is online within 72 hours.On yourname.immerweb.com or your own domain, ready for Google and perfect on mobile.

Frequently asked questions

I only have photos taken on my phone. Is that enough?

Yes. Your phone photos, your Facebook page, Instagram or Google listing are enough to create the film and the website.

Can customers ask me for a quote through the website?

Yes, with a quote request form, a call button and WhatsApp. Requests come straight to you.

Does it suit a shop or a studio?

Yes. Any local business with a place or a craft to show can have its immersive website, or a classic one at the same price.

How quickly will my site be online?
